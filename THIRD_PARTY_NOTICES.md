# Third-Party Notices

## Graphify (preserved snapshot)

The `graphify/` directory in this repository contains a preserved snapshot
of the third-party **Graphify** project. This snapshot was originally
obtained as a ZIP/archive (see `archives/`) and extracted into this
repository.

It is retained here for research purposes, including:

- reverse engineering and study of its architecture,
- comparison against RESILIENT INTELLIGENCE (RI) concepts,
- baseline evaluation, and
- software-codebase validation work for RI research.
