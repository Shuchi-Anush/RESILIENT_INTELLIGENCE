# Contradicting Field Study — Gamma Observatory

## Source: Dr. L. Ortiz, Gamma Longitudinal Field Observatory
## Publication Date: 2024-07-22
## Scope: Field-deployed units of Component X at coastal Site G-7

### Observation GAMMA-9
Over a 14-month real-world exposure at Site G-7 (salt-air, 33°C mean,
91% RH mean), 3 of 20 deployed Component X units exhibited visual
corrosion on electrical pads.

### Claim CONTRADICTS-C2 (direct opposition)
Our field data contradicts the conclusion in analyst_report_2024.md
Claim C2. Specifically:
- C2 asserts "no corrosion-induced failure expected in first 5 years
  under 95% RH / 40°C envelope".
- We observed real corrosion in 15% of a 20-unit sample within 14 months
  under milder RH/temp than the C2 envelope.

Resolution (Dr. Ortiz, 2024-07-22):
The conflict between ALPHA-002 (lab no-corrosion) and GAMMA-9 (field
corrosion observed) is NOT YET RESOLVED. Hypotheses:
(a) lab test TP-3.1 omitted salt aerosol present at G-7;
(b) manufacturing batch variation between the lab lot and G-7 lot;
(c) analyst handbook conversion is too optimistic for salt-laden air.

Until resolution:
- C2 should be treated as INVALIDATED / REQUIRES-REVALIDATION for
  any release decision that covers coastal deployment sites.
- C1 is NOT directly contradicted by GAMMA-9 (thermal durability and
  vibration durability are unchallenged by this field study).
